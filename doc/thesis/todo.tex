\section*{Editorial Section}
This section is for editing purposes only and will not be present in the final document. Additional editing comments are listed on the main body \todo{in red}. For submission refer to \href{https://ppgee.poli.usp.br/pb/formularios/}{this link}.

This document is modeled using \href{https://github.com/lfochamon/poliTeX}{poliTeX} which was discontinued in 2023/01/12 but is more recent than \href{https://github.com/brunocfp/capa-epusp-abntex2}{capa-epusp-abntex2} and \href{http://www.ime.usp.br/~leofl/tex/modelo_poli.zip}{the version I used previously}. This document (as well as poliTeX) primarily follows \href{https://doi.org/10.11606/9786598386221}{USP's official guidelines} (hereafter referred to as UOG) with \href{https://www.poli.usp.br/bibliotecas/servicos/publicacoes-online/}{Poli's complements}. Additionally, \href{https://leginf.usp.br/?resolucao=resolucao-copgr-no-7658-de-20-de-maio-de-2019}{PPGEE's regulations} and \href{https://leginf.usp.br/?resolucao=resolucao-no-7493-de-27-de-marco-de-2018}{USP's regulations} were considered, but state very little about the contents of the written thesis.

The text follows standard logical structure:
Theme Contextualization $\rightarrow$
Literature Review $\rightarrow$
Hypothesis $\rightarrow$
Justification $\rightarrow$
Objective $\rightarrow$
Method $\rightarrow$
Results $\rightarrow$
Conclusions. 
As per UOG (pg. 40) sections ``Introdução'', ``Desenvolvimento'' and ``Conclusão'' must be included, but they may have different names. \href{https://leginf.usp.br/?resolucao=resolucao-copgr-no-7658-de-20-de-maio-de-2019#:~:text=XI.2,Ap%C3%AAndices}{PPGEE's regulations Section XI.2} lists the required sections in a manner incoherent to standard scientific procedure and the UOG (such as defining the objective before the literature review) and as such will be ignored.

Some of the poliTeX inclusions contradict UOG:
\begin{itemize}[nolistsep,noitemsep]
  \item it includes a ``falsa folha de rosto'' not mentioned in any guideline. It will be ignored.
  \item it does not include ``departamento'' in the title page (despite adding a variable for such). It will be added manually.
\end{itemize}
